HOW TO PLAY
  • Mouse: move it and the ship follows. Hold click to fire.
  • Keyboard: ←→ or AD turn, ↑ or W thrust, SPACE or Z fire.
  • Phone: use the on-screen buttons.
  • Clear every rock to finish a wave. Rocks split when hit, so don't get boxed in.
  • Power-ups: S shield, R rapid fire, T triple shot. Fly into them to collect.
  • A boss rock appears every 5th wave and shoots back.
  • P pauses the game.
