gecko-games

Ten games, no installs. Pick one and play right in your browser, on desktop or phone. Add it to your home screen to play offline.

On iPhone or iPad: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen” to install.

Vector Drift

Fly a small ship through drifting rocks. Shoot them and they split, so watch for getting boxed in. Chain hits for combos, grab power-ups, and beat a boss rock every 5th wave.

Arrows or WASD to fly, Space to fire, P to pause. Touch buttons on phones.
Play

Last Frontier

Drop onto an island with your team, scavenge crates, and take out the rival team as the safe zone shrinks. Four maps to choose from.

WASD to move, mouse to look, click to shoot. On phones, turn sideways: left stick moves, drag to look, tap Fire. Squad battles against bots.
Play

Cosmic Blaster

Shoot down waves of alien ships, dodge their fire, and take on a boss. Collect shield, rapid-fire, and triple-shot power-ups.

Your ship follows the mouse (F toggles it, WASD or arrows work too). Click, Space or Z to shoot, P to pause. On phones, drag to steer and it fires automatically.
Play

Tic Tac Toe

The classic grid showdown with a neon twist. Play against a friend or challenge the AI across three difficulty levels.

Click or tap to place your mark. PvP or vs AI with Easy, Medium, and Hard modes.
Play

Snake

Guide a hungry snake around the grid. Every bite makes you longer, so plan your turns.

Snake follows your mouse or finger. Arrows or WASD also work. P or Space to pause.
Play

Memory Match

Flip cards and find all eight space-themed pairs in as few moves as you can.

Click or tap cards. Best score saved.
Play

Brick Breaker

Arcade brick-smasher with bevelled bricks, silver and gold blocks, falling power-up capsules (wider paddle, slower ball) and new rounds. Bounce the ball off your paddle and clear every brick.

Mouse, touch drag or arrows to move. Click or Space to launch. No extra lives, so one miss ends the run.
Play

2048

Slide and merge numbered tiles until you make 2048. Easy to learn, tough to master.

Arrows, WASD or swipe.
Play

Sky Hop

Tap to flap and squeeze a little bird through endless green pipes. One touch and it's over, so keep your rhythm. Earn bronze, silver and gold medals.

Click, tap or Space.
Play

Dino Run

Sprint across the desert, leap over cacti and duck under pterodactyls. The faster you go, the tighter it gets.

Space, W or Up to jump, Down or S to duck. Tap the top of the screen to jump, hold the bottom to duck.
